Changes to Dereth

Every change to the world, newest first, with the reason. Each one is also posted to #patch-notes-content in Discord the moment it goes live. Experience comes in at half the retail rate everywhere — the level cap is 500 and enlightenment is meant to be repeated, so the climb is the game and the shortcuts around it are the first things to go.

The features that are always on — the currencies, the bank, personal dungeons, enlightenment on our terms — are on the front page. This page is for what changed, and when.

11 September 2026

Personal dungeons are live, starting with the Egg Orchard

Your own copy, scaled by your enlightenment. See What's different above.

11 September 2026

Three free experience sources removed

The casino's Wheel of Knowledge is closed — the High-Stakes Gamesmasters no longer take Ancient Empyrean or Falatacot Trinkets. The Writ of Apology is no longer handed out, Experience Orders are no longer honored, and the Royal Quartermaster no longer stocks them. The Experience Certificate is gone — Marid no longer sells it and the Arcanum no longer honors it. In every case the NPC politely refuses and you keep the item.

11 September 2026

Tusk and pincer turn-ins pay a quarter of retail

All fifteen Tusker tusks at Brighteyes the Tailor, and the Brood Matron Pincer at the Olthoi Hunter. One Snow Tusker Leader Tusk was 95 million on its own; it's now 23.75 million. The Sharp Tusker Slave Tusk hand-in, the Prodigal Tusker's Token, and Jilna Fullgood's hand-ins are unchanged.

Aeshnidae runs on ACEmulator with its own set of server mods, hosted on a dedicated server in Montreal. Only the game ports are open to the internet. Player data is saved continuously, backed up every half hour, and copies are kept off the server.
